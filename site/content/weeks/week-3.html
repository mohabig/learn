Until now your system answers. This week it acts — it runs a query, writes a file, searches the web. That shift is smaller than it sounds, because a model still only emits text. And it is more dangerous than it sounds, because text pulled off a web page can now steer what your code does.

You start with the bare mechanism of tool calling. Then you wrap it in an agent loop with a fence around it. Then comes the harder judgment: most tasks do not need an agent at all, and knowing which ones do is worth more than any framework. On Day 17 your Week-2 corpus becomes a tool that any client can call.

Days 18 and 19 are the center of the whole month. You read fifty of your own failures and label them by hand. You build a judge, then check the judge against yourself. Then you wire a regression gate into CI, so a worse prompt blocks your own merge. Day 20 you attack your own agent on purpose. Day 21 you ship the whole thing.

By the end of the week you can say what your system does, how often it does it right, and what happens when someone tries to trick it.
