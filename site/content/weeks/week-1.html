Week 1 is the layer everything else sits on: one call to a model, and everything you can know about it.

Here is the target. By Sunday you can look at any request your code makes and say four things out loud — what it cost, how long it took, where the time went, and what it does when it fails. Most people who have used these APIs for a year cannot say any of the four.

You start with the raw SDK. No framework, on purpose, because a framework hides exactly the numbers you came here to learn: tokens, temperature, time to the first word versus time to the last. Then the week builds. Day 2 moves prompts out of your code and into versioned files with test cases. Day 3 is structured output, the highest-return day of the week: typed objects instead of prose you have to pick apart with regular expressions. Day 4 puts whole documents in the prompt and makes them cheap with caching. Day 5 streams and caches. Day 6 breaks everything on purpose. Day 7 you deploy it and write down the numbers.
