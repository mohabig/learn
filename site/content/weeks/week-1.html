This week you meet the model: a computer program that uses patterns from examples to produce text. You will try asking it questions, give it clearer instructions, and notice that the same question can lead to different answers.

New words will appear, but you do not need to memorise them before they make sense. A token is a numbered piece of text. A prompt is the instruction and information you give the model. A schema is a checklist for the shape of an answer. Each idea arrives with an example you can inspect.

Some model services need an account and may cost money. Start with the offline examples when possible; ask a trusted adult before signing up, entering a key, or spending anything. Use imaginary characters and made-up examples. At the end of a session, jot down one thing that surprised you and one question you still have.
