Three projects are deployed, and you have a habit of measuring things. Week 4 turns that habit into proof — the kind a stranger can check in six minutes.

You start by turning the lights on. Every model call gets recorded: inputs, outputs, tokens, cost, latency, tool calls, retries. Then two days of judgment. Day 23 you fine-tune something for real, once, so you can say from data where it wins and where it loses. Day 24 you run a model on your own machine and find out what shrinking it actually costs you.

Days 25 to 27 are the capstone: one product you would personally use every week, built out of everything — retrieval, tools, evals, tracing, auth, deploy. Day 28 you push it until it bends and write down what you would do about each way it breaks. Day 29 is the write-up, and it is the highest-leverage day of the month. Day 30 aims all of it at a job.

So: build the thing, measure the thing, then hand someone the receipts.
