This week is about looking closely at what your project does. Keep a simple notebook: what did you try, what happened, what surprised you, and what might you change? Do not save private information in logs.

You can explore a model that runs on your own computer, if an adult helps decide whether the download and your device are suitable. You do not need special hardware or paid services to finish the main course. There are optional challenges for learners who want to understand how smaller models trade space and speed for some accuracy.

Then return to the project you chose. Pick one useful improvement, test it with a few examples, and make a simple diagram or explanation. You can show it to someone you trust; sharing online is optional and needs adult agreement. There is no job application, interview, public launch, or perfect-score requirement.

So: make something you care about, learn what it can and cannot do, and decide what you are curious about next.
