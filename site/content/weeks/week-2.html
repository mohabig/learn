This week you give your project something useful to read. Choose a small collection of public, fictional, or adult-approved notes about a topic you like. Your program will split the notes into pieces and try to find the ones that might help with a question.

One search method looks for the same words. Another tries to find similar meaning. Both can make mistakes. You will compare them using examples you wrote yourself, then see whether showing the right passages helps the model answer more carefully.

The important discovery is not a score or a fancy database. It is learning to ask: “Did it find the right clue? Can I point to the note that supports this answer?” Technical names such as embeddings and retrieval come after you have something concrete to explore.
